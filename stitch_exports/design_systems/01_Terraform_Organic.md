## Brand & Style
The design system is rooted in the intersection of traditional agriculture and modern technology. It targets field operators, agronomists, and farm managers who require high clarity under varied lighting conditions.

The aesthetic is **Organic Minimalism**: a blend of clean, functional layouts and a color story derived from nature. It utilizes generous whitespace to reduce cognitive load during complex field tasks.
