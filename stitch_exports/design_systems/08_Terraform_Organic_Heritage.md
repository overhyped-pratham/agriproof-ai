## Brand & Style
The design system is an evolution of organic minimalism, now infused with the vibrant visual heritage of Indian agriculture. It targets a demographic that balances traditional wisdom with modern data-driven farming.

The aesthetic transition moves from "clinical stewardship" to "cultural celebration," evoking the warmth of a sun-drenched field and the reliability of earth-bound traditions.
